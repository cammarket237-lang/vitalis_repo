🔒 Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 14, 2026
Our Privacy Commitment

CamMarket237 is committed to protecting your personal information. We collect only what is necessary to provide our services.

What We Collect
  • Name and phone number (for account creation)
  • Town and region (for local listings)
  • GPS location (only when you choose to share)
  • Photos and videos you post
  • Device type and IP address (for security)
How We Use Your Data
  • To create and manage your account
  • To show relevant listings near you
  • To facilitate safe buyer-seller meetings
  • To send important notifications
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
We Never
  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Share your phone number without your consent
  • Store your password in plain text
  • Track your location without your permission
Your Rights
  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
Security

We use bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS encryption, and rate limiting to protect your data.

Children

CamMarket237 is not for users under 18 years of age.

📋 Terms of Service
Last updated: May 14, 2026
About CamMarket237

CamMarket237 is a connector platform that links buyers and sellers in Cameroon. We do not buy, sell, or hold any goods. All transactions are directly between buyers and sellers.

Eligibility
  • You must be at least 18 years old
  • You must provide accurate information
  • One buyer + one seller account per phone number
Seller Rules
  • All photos must show the actual item
  • Prices must be honest and in FCFA
  • Condition must be accurately stated
  • No fake, stolen, or illegal items
  • No adult content or weapons
Buyer Rules
  • Never pay before inspecting the item
  • Always use our safety meeting system
  • Report suspicious sellers immediately
Prohibited
  • Fake accounts or impersonation
  • Fraud or misleading listings
  • Harassment of other users
  • Spam or unsolicited messages
Referral Points
  • Referrer earns 5 points per successful referral
  • New user earns 10 points for using a code
  • Points cannot be exchanged for cash
Limitation of Liability

CamMarket237 is not responsible for disputes, losses, or damages arising from transactions between users. Use the platform at your own risk.

Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Cameroon.

🛡️ Safety Guide
Read before meeting any buyer or seller
✅ ALWAYS DO THIS
  • Meet in a public place (market, mall, police station)
  • Bring a friend or family member
  • Tell someone where you are going
  • Inspect the item before paying
  • Use our "We Are Together" GPS feature
  • Trust your instincts — leave if unsafe
❌ NEVER DO THIS
  • Never pay before seeing the item
  • Never meet in a private home (first time)
  • Never meet alone at night
  • Never send transport money
  • Never share your OTP or PIN
  • Never pay by unusual methods (gift cards, crypto)
🚨 Red Flag Warning Signs
  • Price is suspiciously too low
  • Seller asks for deposit or advance payment
  • Refuses to meet in public
  • Photos look like stock images
  • New account with no reviews
  • Asks for OTP code or personal ID
  • "My agent will come to collect"
